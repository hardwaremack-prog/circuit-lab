# Circuit Lab

A live breadboard and PCB simulator with 20 guided projects, from your first LED to a finished board you can export.

![Circuit Lab screenshot](<Circuit Lab - screenshot.png>)

## What's in it

- **Real simulation.** Wires carry current, LEDs light up (and burn out if you overdrive them), capacitors charge and discharge in real time
- **17 components**: batteries, resistors, switches, LEDs, potentiometers, capacitors, 555 timers, transistors, MOSFETs, relays, diodes, inductors, photoresistors, solar cells and more
- **20 projects** covering series and parallel circuits, timers, transistors, AC power and bridge rectifiers, solar power, and real PCB design with vias
- Every project has a goal, a hint, and a **Cheat** button that shows a working schematic
- **Schematic** draws a clean diagram from whatever you actually built
- **PCB mode** and **Export Design** download your board as an SVG plus a parts and connection list
- Save your circuits, tilt the board in 3D, and mute or unmute the sounds

## How to use it

Click a part in the bin on the left to add it. Drag a part to move it, drag from pin to pin to wire, click a wire to delete it, and tap a part to change its value.

## Run it

Download `circuit-lab-b10.html` and open it in your web browser. No install, no account, nothing to set up.

The full guide is in `circuit-lab-2-manual.pdf`.
